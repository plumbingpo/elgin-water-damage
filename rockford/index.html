Restoration Challenges Rockford Homeowners Face

In Rockford, the same handful of problems accounts for nearly all the water damage we see. If you can spot these early, you have a good chance of stopping the damage before it spreads.

Pipes: A pipe that freezes can split, and the flooding starts once the ice thaws and pressure returns. Lines in exterior walls, garages and unheated attics are the usual trouble spots, depending on the season. In older homes, aging joints and tired connectors sometimes fail with no cold snap involved. Your first move is closing the main water valve, since a broken pipe floods room after room fast.

Appliances: Washing machines, dishwashers and refrigerator ice makers all depend on small hoses that wear out. Tanks rust from the inside, so an old water heater may drip for weeks or give out suddenly. These failures do the most harm when the appliance is on an upper floor or above finished living space.

Overflows: It takes only a few minutes for a blocked toilet or a forgotten faucet to flood a bathroom. From there it slips beneath the trim and can drip into whatever sits under that floor. Water that backed up out of a drain or toilet bowl is not clean, so keep kids and pets out of it.

Roof: Rain gets in through worn flashing, damaged shingles or gutters that have stopped draining. By the time a brown ring appears overhead, the attic insulation has often been wet for a while. We dry the framing, insulation and drywall, and a roofer needs to fix the opening so it does not return.

Basement: In homes that have a basement, groundwater pushes through foundation cracks and floor joints when the soil is saturated. If the sump pump sticks or the electricity goes out mid-storm, the pit fills and spills across the slab. Finished basements suffer most, since carpet, drywall and framing all soak up water from the bottom.

Mold: Mold can start growing within 24 to 48 hours on materials that stay wet. A lot of the mold we find in Rockford comes from a past leak that looked dry on the surface only. A musty smell, peeling paint or dark spotting near a previous repair is worth having checked.
